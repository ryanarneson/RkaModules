HADAL

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Hadal is a rumble and noise bed with no input: low rumble, hiss and crackle, darkened together by one Depth dial (0 to 11 km) and kept moving by Drift, each layer on its own jack and all three mixed to L and R. It is meant to sit under a piece and feed other modules, such as Fog, filters and delays. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: how the panel looks, the tooltips and the hover text on the jacks. The ranges, the defaults and the levels are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

Verdicts (first guesses; say what to change)

WhatThe question
DEPTH RANGEHiss runs from 12 kHz down to 1.5 kHz, Rumble from 160 Hz down to 70 Hz (growl 20 to 70 Hz). Is 0 km too bright, or 11 km not dark enough, for dark ambient work?
RUMBLE SOFT CLIPRumble is soft-clipped, harder as Depth goes down to 11 km, so about a third of its energy there is over 60 Hz. Does that keep it audible without turning it buzzy, or should the clip be gentler?
DEFAULTSRumble 80 %, Hiss 30 %, Crackle 0 %, Drift 25 %, Depth noon. A new Hadal gives a bed at once with no crackle. Keep, or start with a little Crackle?
CRACKLE LEVELAt full, its own jack carries about 0.14 V RMS; the ticks are soft-clipped, and at Drift 0 each side of the mix stays inside ±5 V, but the CRACKLE jack itself reaches 6.8 V at 0 km (it is the mono sum of both sides). Drift can lift the odd tick on a side to about 5.3 V at the default Drift, 7.6 V at full (the jack: 6.9 V and 8.8 V). Is full enough, too quiet beside Hiss, or are the loud ticks too loud?
DRIFT25 % by default; the pace is fixed (a new destination every 8 to 30 s) with no knob for it. Is the pace right for ambient work, and is 25 % too little, too much?
LEVEL LAWThe three level knobs are squared (half a turn is 12 dB down). Does the travel feel even, or is the bottom crowded?
MONO THROUGH LWith R unpatched, rumble is about 3 dB louder than in the stereo mix, hiss unchanged, crackle about 2 dB louder. Fine, or should rumble match?
POLYPHONY (#64)Hadal is monophonic: a polyphonic CV uses channel 1. Is that enough, or is a poly output wanted?

Patches

1. Hadal into Fog

Hadal L → Fog left input · Hadal R → Fog right input · Fog L, R → Ballast channel 1 left, right

Fog on a long Visibility with some Bloom. Turn DEPTH down for something heavier, up for air. A space that is never empty.

2. Hiss through a filter

Hadal HISS → Narrows L input · Sway TRI (slow) → Narrows FM CV · Narrows L → Ballast channel 1

Turn Narrows FM up and some Resonance. Wind, surf, a breath through a hull. Hiss leaves the L/R mix while HISS is patched, so patch L and R elsewhere to keep the rest of the bed.

3. Crackle into a delay

Hadal CRACKLE → Wake L input · Wake L, R → Fog left, right · Hadal L, R → Ballast channel 2

Turn CRACKLE up (on its own jack it is sparse and fairly quiet). Every tick leaves a trail. The L/R mix still carries Rumble and Hiss.

4. Depth from a slow LFO

Sway SIN (Rate low) → Hadal DEPTH CV · Hadal L, R → Fog

Depth's attenuverter at about a third clockwise. The bed sinks and rises over minutes. Try the attenuverter anticlockwise: it rises when the LFO falls.

5. Distant thunder

Squall GATE → Beacon GATE · Beacon OUT → Sluice A level CV · Hadal RUMBLE → Sluice A in · Sluice D → Fog left input

Squall Weather near Fair, Gust 60 %. Hadal Depth fully right, Rumble full. Each gate swells the rumble in and lets it fade.

6. Drift from the weather

Squall SWELL → Hadal DRIFT CV · Squall STEP → Hadal CRACKLE CV · Hadal L, R → Fog

Both attenuverters about a quarter clockwise, Hadal Crackle at about 30 %. The bed's movement and its crackle follow Squall's slow weather.
