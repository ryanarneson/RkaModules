BALLAST

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Ballast is a 12 HP four-channel stereo mixer: each channel has a Level knob and a level CV that works like a VCA, the four sum through Master with a CHAIN input, and the output is DC-blocked and soft-limited. Pan is its 6 HP expander: a pan knob, attenuverter and CV for each channel, with true pan or balance for stereo sources and a choice of pan law. 2.0.39 is the build the listening list is cut at; Pan is added from Add Pan expander in Ballast's right-click menu. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: the lights, tooltips, the menus, LINK and how the panels look. The Level curve, the limiter's knee and the Pan defaults are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

Verdicts (first guesses; say what to change)

WhatThe question
LEVEL CURVEUnity at three quarters, +6 dB fully right, about -8.5 dB at noon and -23 dB at a quarter turn. Is the travel even to your hand, and is unity at three quarters the right place for a default?
CV CAN'T BOOSTA level CV opens from silence at 0 V to the knob's level at 10 V and never goes past the knob. Right for envelopes and tremolo, or should a CV be able to go above the knob?
LIMITER AND CLIPNothing below 8 V is touched, nothing leaves above 10 V, and CLIP holds 150 ms. Is 8 V the right knee, and does CLIP warn you early enough?
DC BLOCKER (#44)On by default, 3 Hz. Turning it on fades an offset over 50 ms; turning it off steps. Fade the off step too, or leave it as the right behaviour on CV?
PAN LAW DEFAULTNo boost by default: a source is a little quieter at the sides (about 3 dB for mono), but cannot push a clean mix into the limiter. For dark, quiet work is that the right default, or should Constant loudness be?
STEREO SOURCESTrue pan is the default: the whole image moves and nothing is lost. Does Balance suit your stereo sources better?
ATTENUVERTER AT 0Each Pan attenuverter starts at 0, so a patched CV does nothing until you turn it. Keep, or start at +100 %?
GLIDE ON LOAD (#42)Pan glides in from centre on patch load (63 % in 10 ms, settled in about 85 ms) and on attach. Is it audible and does it matter, given the spec wanted a snap on load?

Patches

1. Four sources and a reverb

Fathom Left / Right → Ballast IN 1 L / R · Hadal Hiss → IN 2 L · Ballast L / R → Fog Left / Right

Level 1 and 2 at unity, Hiss low. The stereo Fathom keeps its image; the mono Hiss sits in the centre. Turn Master and watch CLIP.

2. Slow auto-pan

Hadal Hiss → Ballast IN 1 L · Sway Sine → Pan PAN 1 CV · Ballast L / R → Fog Left / Right

Right-click Ballast, Add Pan expander. Turn Pan 1's attenuverter up and set Sway's Rate slow: the noise drifts left and right. Try each Pan law and listen for the dip at the extremes.

3. Ducking and swelling

Fathom Left → Ballast IN 1 L · Squall Gate → Beacon GATE · Beacon OUT → Ballast CV 1

Level 1 at unity. Each gate swells the channel from silence to the knob. The swell never goes above the knob.

4. Two Ballasts, one Pan each

Ballast A L / R → Ballast B CHAIN L / R · Ballast B L / R → Fog Left / Right

Add a Pan to each Ballast from its menu. Pan A's four channels and Pan B's four channels separately. Turn Ballast B's Master and hear all eight move.

5. Pan stereo against mono

Fathom Left / Right → IN 1 L / R · Fathom Sub → IN 2 L · Pan 1 and Pan 2 CV from one slow Sway Sine

Pan 1 is a stereo source, Pan 2 a mono one. Switch Stereo sources between True pan and Balance and listen to how only channel 1 changes.

6. Unlinking test

Hadal Hiss → Ballast IN 1 L · Ballast L / R → Fog

Turn Pan 1 hard right with the Hiss running. Drag Pan away, then back; bypass it and un-bypass it. Listen for the step to centre and the glide in (settled in about 85 ms), and watch LINK.

7. Ballast as a CV mixer

Sway Triangle → Ballast IN 1 L · Sway Saw → IN 2 L · Ballast L → Fathom Cutoff CV

Switch the DC blocker off in Ballast's menu so slow offsets pass. Level 1 and 2 set how much of each LFO reaches the filter. Turning the blocker back on fades the offset away.
