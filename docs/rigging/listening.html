RIGGING

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Rigging is six tuned strings in 14 HP, after wind in a ship's rigging. Any sound you patch in keeps the strings ringing at their pitches, so noise becomes a tuned drone, and a trigger at STRUM (or a new note at V/OCT) strikes the strings, one after another from the lowest. Both can run at once on the same strings: a drone underneath and strikes on top. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: the strike light, tooltips, the menu and how the panel looks. The detunes, the string positions, Ring and Damp's laws and the pitch glide are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

Verdicts (first guesses; say what to change)

WhatThe question
DEFAULT CHORDFifths, with Unison to Bell in seven stops. Is Fifths the right place to start for dark, ambient work, and is the order of the seven right?
RING0.1 s to 60 s, default 4 s, the time shown at Damp 0. Is the default long enough for drones, and is the top long enough?
DAMPDefault is a third, so a 60 s Ring still lasts over 30 s; fully right nothing rings more than about two seconds. Is the curve even, and is the default too bright or too dark?
DETUNESUnison is -8, -4, 0, +3, +7, +11 cents; Octaves has the doubled strings 5 cents apart. Too much beating, too little, or right?
STRING POSITIONSString 1 in the middle; the others at -0.25, +0.25, -0.5, +0.5, -0.75 on alternate sides. Is the stereo picture wide enough, and too lopsided?
PITCH GLIDEA change of pitch closes on its target with an 8 ms time constant (the spec first suggested 30 ms). Does a chord or Tune change sound smooth, or should it be slower?
SPREAD0 to 1.5 s with the default 60 ms. Is a quick strum the right default, and is 1.5 s long enough for a slow rake?
NOTE STRIKES (#54)With STRUM unpatched, a jump of more than 1/30 V within 1 ms strikes, so a fast wide LFO on V/OCT can strike. Add a menu item to turn it off, or require V/OCT to settle first?

Patches

1. A tuned drone

Hadal Hiss → Rigging IN L · Rigging L / R → Fog Left / Right

Chord at Fifths or Unison, Ring long, Damp about half. Tune it under a Fathom or by ear.

2. Distant bells

Squall Gate → Rigging STRUM · Rigging L / R → Fog Left / Right

Chord at Bell, Ring about 20 s, Spread about a third. Fog's weather near Fair.

3. A plucked line from one cable

Shoal A → Rigging V/OCT (STRUM empty) · Rigging L / R → Fog Left / Right

Each new note strikes. Ring 1 to 2 s and Spread at 0. Then patch a trigger into STRUM and watch the note-strikes stop.

4. Breathing brightness

Hadal Hiss → Rigging IN L · Sway Triangle → Rigging Damp CV

Slow Sway Rate. The drone opens and closes as its upper overtones come and go.

5. Harmony that moves

Hadal Hiss → Rigging IN L · Squall Step → Rigging Chord CV

The tuning changes with each Squall event. Try Squall's smaller ranges so it steps between neighbours.

6. A drone that swells in

Hadal Hiss → Sluice A in · Squall Gate → Beacon GATE · Beacon OUT → Sluice A level CV · Sluice A out → Rigging IN L

Open Sluice's Level A. Each Squall gate swells the noise into the strings and lets it fall away; Ring sets how long the drone hangs on.

7. Pitched input

Fathom Left → Rigging IN L · the same V/OCT to Fathom V/OCT and Rigging V/OCT

A pitched sound makes the strings near its pitch sing loudest. Mix at noon blends the Fathom and the strings.
