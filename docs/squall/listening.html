SQUALL

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Squall is a random source of gates and CV, the weather of a piece. One dial, Weather, sets how much happens, from one event in ten minutes to eight a second; Gust bunches events into bursts, Memory replays the recent past, and the three outputs (GATE, STEP, SWELL) tell one story. It runs free or locks to a clock. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: how the panel looks, the tooltips, the event light and the right-click menu. Gust's strength, Swell's pace and the defaults are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

WhatThe question
WEATHER SCALEOne event in 10 minutes at Calm, 72 s at Fair, 8.7 s at noon, about one a second at Rain, 8 a second at Stormy. Is noon the right default for ambient work, and is the range wide enough at both ends?
GUSTDefault 30 %. Full is strong but never changes the average rate. Are the bursts clear enough at full, too strong at 30 %?
GATE LENGTHA quarter of the average gap, held to 5 ms to 4 s: about 2.2 s at noon, 4 s at Calm and Fair, 30 ms at Stormy. Fine for strikes and slow envelopes, or should the fraction change?
SWELL PACEIt reaches a new value in about one average gap, so about 9 s at noon, tens of minutes at Calm. Right for drones, too slow or too fast against Weather?
MEMORYLoops of 4, 8 (default) or 16 events, saved with the patch. Are the three lengths enough, and does 70 % read as a pattern that wears away?
CLOCK AT STORMYAt Stormy every tick fires, at any Gust, once it is running (the spec says so). Only a start-up lull of up to 26 ticks at Gust 1 skips ticks. The manual's line about skipped ticks is wrong. Is the start-up lull fine?
RANGES±5 V by default, with 0 to 10 V, ±2 V and ±1 V in the menu. Is ±5 V the right default, and are these four enough?
POLYPHONY (#64)Squall is one weather system: a polyphonic CV uses channel 1, and two Squalls make two. Enough, or is a poly output wanted?

Patches

1. Distant thunder

Squall GATE → Beacon GATE · Beacon OUT → Sluice A level CV · Hadal RUMBLE → Sluice A in · Sluice D → Fog left input

Weather near Fair, Gust 60 %. Each gate swells the rumble in and lets it fade. Beacon's A and R set how.

2. A drone that breathes

Squall SWELL (Range 0 to 10 V) → Ballast channel 1 level CV · Fathom Left, Right → Ballast channel 1 left, right

Weather about a quarter left of noon. The drone's level drifts at a pace that follows the weather.

3. Moving room

Squall SWELL → Fog Bloom CV (attenuverter about a third) · Hadal L, R → Fog left, right

Swell moves Fog's Bloom slowly and never jumps.

4. Wandering pan

Squall SWELL → Pan 1 CV on a Pan expander beside Ballast

Use one Squall for each channel. The Range menu's ±1 V or ±2 V gives a narrow wander.

5. A loop that wears away

Squall GATE → Rigging STRUM · Squall STEP (Range ±1 V) → Rigging V/OCT · Rigging L, R → Fog

Memory 100 %, Loop length 4: a four-note phrase repeats exactly. Bring Memory down to about 60 % and it wears away.

6. A storm across a piece

Sway SIN (Rate very low) → Squall Gust CV and Weather CV · Squall GATE → Narrows PING

Both attenuverters about halfway clockwise. The piece goes from calm and steady to stormy and bunched, and back.

7. Clocked and frozen

Sway SQR (Rate about 2 Hz) → Squall CLOCK · a gate → Squall FREEZE · Squall GATE → Narrows PING · Hadal HISS → Narrows L

Weather near Rain: events fall only on the clock's ticks, about one in four. Hold FREEZE and everything stops; let go and it continues.
