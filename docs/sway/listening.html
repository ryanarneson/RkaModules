SWAY

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Sway is a polyphonic LFO, 12 HP: sine, triangle, saw and square outputs, a MORPH output you shape with MORPH, SKEW and SMOOTH, a random output (stepped, or gliding in), and an end-of-cycle trigger. It runs from 4.5 hours per cycle up to audio rate, can follow a clock, and has a one-shot mode that turns it into an envelope. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: the rate light, the one-shot light, tooltips, the Range menu and how the panel looks. The three ranges, the defaults and the random output's behaviour are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

WhatThe question
DEFAULT RATENormal range, RATE at +1: 2 Hz. For ambient and dark work, is that too fast to start from? Should the default sit at 0.1 Hz or slower, or should Slow be the default range?
THE THREE RANGESSlow runs 4.5 h to 1 s per cycle (RATE 0 is about 2 min 8 s), Normal 0.008 to 128 Hz, Audio about 8 Hz to 8.4 kHz. Are the ends right? Is the Normal top (128 Hz) useful, or would Slow reaching 4 Hz be better?
SMOOTH DOES TWO JOBSLeft of centre it rounds MORPH's corners and also makes RAND glide in; right of centre it folds MORPH. Is one knob for both fine, or does rounding MORPH when you only wanted RAND to glide get in the way?
RANDA new uniform value in ±5 V each cycle, stepped at centre and right, gliding in from the left. Each channel has its own sequence, but the seeds are fixed, so two Sways started together play the same RAND values. Is that acceptable?
PHASE AND RANDThe manual says PHASE shifts every output. In the code RAND and EOC are read from the cycle itself, so PHASE (and SPREAD) never move them. Intended? If yes, the manual line needs a fix.
CV SCALEOn PW, PHASE, SKEW, SMOOTH and MORPH, 5 V at full attenuverter sweeps the whole knob range. Right for LFO-on-LFO, or too sensitive for slow work?
RATE LIGHTIt blinks (lit for the first half of each cycle) up to 10 Hz, then glows steadily at half brightness. Is 10 Hz the right switch point, and is it readable at 0.01 Hz?

Patches

1. Slow breath

Sway SIN (Range Slow, RATE about +1) → Sluice A level CV · Fathom L → Sluice A

Sluice's level A knob at about 50 %. The drone swells and fades over about a minute. Switch BI / UNI to UNI and compare: 0 to 10 V is the whole level range.

2. Wandering cutoff

Sway RAND (SMOOTH fully left) → Narrows FM · Fathom L → Narrows Left

Narrows FM knob about 30 %. With SMOOTH fully left, RAND glides over the whole cycle, so the cutoff drifts from one random value to the next without a step. Move SMOOTH to centre and it steps instead.

3. Sway clocks Sway

Sway 1 SQR → Sway 2 CLOCK · Sway 2 SIN and TRI → a scope

After two edges Sway 2 follows Sway 1; set RATE on Sway 2 to ×2, ÷4 and watch the cycles. Patch Sway 1 FM from Sway 2 to make it wobble.

4. One-shot envelope

Squall GATE → Sway RESET · Sway TRI (UNI) → Sluice B level CV · Sway EOC → Rigging STRUM

Switch on 1-SHOT and BI / UNI to UNI, and set PHASE to 0.75 (270°). Each Squall gate runs one triangle hump over Sluice, 0 → 10 → 0 V, and TRI rests at 0 V. (With PHASE at 0, TRI runs 5 → 10 → 0 → 5 V and rests at 5 V, which holds Sluice half open.) EOC strikes Rigging when the hump ends (PHASE does not move EOC). Clock edges would not restart it; only RESET does.

5. Spread across four

Sway SIN (CHANNELS 4, SPREAD 100 %) → Scope (4 channels)

Four sines a quarter of a cycle apart. Lower SPREAD to bring them together. Patch the poly cable into another poly input to modulate four things at once.

6. Freeze on a gate

Squall GATE → Sway HOLD · Sway SIN → Narrows FM

While the gate is high the modulation stops where it is and the rate light stays put. Releasing resumes from the same place.

7. Sway as an oscillator

Sway SAW (Range Audio) → Narrows Left · Narrows Left out → Fog Left

RATE sets the pitch (centred on C4); FM is 1 V per octave at +100 %. SAW and SQR are band-limited here.
