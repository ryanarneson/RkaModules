# Fog Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fog, a 10 HP stereo reverb in the Instrument look. It makes enormous, dark, slowly blooming spaces in the style of Valhalla Supermassive and Room's LV-426. It has a time-based Visibility hero, five CV-able knobs, and four modes chosen from the right-click menu.

**Architecture:**
- **Engine:** a Rack-free `rka::FogEngine` (`src/fog/FogEngine.hpp`) runs a multichannel diffuser into a feedback delay network. Each mode is a `Voicing`, a set of parameters for that one engine.
- **Shared delay line:** a Rack-free delay line in `src/DelayLine.hpp`, for Wake and Eddy to reuse later.
- **Module:** `Fog.cpp` only maps ports, params and menu settings onto the engine, as `Sluice.cpp` does.
- **Panel:** a `"Fog"` entry in `tools/gen_panels.py`, which gains one face feature: words placed at given turns.

**Tech Stack:** C++11 plugin code (Rack SDK 2.6.6), C++17 offline tests with `src/testing.hpp`, the Python 3 panel generator, and Inkscape 1.4.4.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-15-fog-design.md`, with its mockup `docs/specs/2026-11-15-fog-mockup.svg`. Testing rules: `TESTING.md`. Read the spec before any task. Where this plan and the spec disagree, the spec wins; report the difference.

## Global Constraints

- **Build:** with `RACK_DIR=$HOME/ws/sdk/Rack-SDK-2.6.6-mac-arm64`. Agents never start VCV Rack except through `make smoke`. `make install` is allowed (it copies the plugin into the user's Rack folder for their listening checks).
- **Voltages:** audio is ±5 V. A ±5 V input gives a wet signal of about ±5 V at noon settings. CV is ±5 V at full attenuverter and sweeps the knob's whole range, added to the knob and clamped to [0, 1]. Attenuverters default to 0.
- **Defaults:** every main knob defaults to 0.5 (noon).
- **Visibility:** `T60 = 0.4 s × 200^v`, v in [0, 1]. The words sit at CLEAR 0.042, HAZE 0.304, MIST 0.477, FOG 0.642, THICK 0.815 and DENSE 0.975 (as v). Every per-line gain is below 1, so the tail always decays. Visibility has no CV.
- **Size:** scales every line's length by about 0.3× to 3× on a log scale, slewed over about 100 ms. Size does not change T60.
- **Damp:** in HAAR, the ratio of the high (about 4 kHz) T60 to the low T60 is about 1 fully left, 0.5 at noon and 0.125 fully right. Visibility means the low-frequency decay.
- **Bloom:** in HAAR, the rise to within 3 dB of steady of the signal fed into the network is under 50 ms fully left, about 1 s at noon and about 4 s fully right. It acts on the wet path only, and the dry path has zero delay.
- **Drift:** fully left is no modulation. Fully right gives pitch deviations in the tens of cents. Its rate is a menu setting: Slow (about 0.1–0.5 Hz) or Very slow (about 5× slower).
- **Mix:** equal-power. 0 is dry only, 1 is wet only. Dry kill forces wet only, overriding the knob and CV.
- **Wet level** is evened out across Visibility: a held drone's wet level stays within ±3 dB from CLEAR to DENSE.
- **Limiter:** on the wet signal only, keeping it within about ±10 V. It does not act on a ±5 V input. The dry signal is never touched.
- **Low cut** applies to the network's input: Off, 50 Hz (default) or 100 Hz.
- **Ports:** IN R normals to IN L. Polyphonic inputs are summed per side. Outputs are mono per side. Bypass is IN L → OUT L and IN R → OUT R.
- **Modes:** HAAR (default), FRET, SMOKE and ICE, chosen in the menu only.
  - Switching: the wet signal fades out over about 50 ms, the network is cleared, and the new mode starts clean.
  - Knob meanings are the same in every mode. The numeric Damp and Bloom targets are exact in HAAR; other modes start from their own baseline.
- **Internal rate:** capped at 96 kHz. Above it, the network runs at the host rate halved, once or more, using `src/fathom/Halfband.hpp`'s up- and downsamplers. Buffers are allocated only on construction and on a sample-rate change, never in `process()`.
- **Hostile values:** NaN and ±inf on any input or param become 0 where they enter. A sample rate that is not finite or is ≤ 0 is ignored, and the last good rate is kept, as Sluice does.
- **Engine rules:** no libm call per sample (use `src/FastMath.hpp`), no allocation in `process()`, no writable globals, denormals handled. Plugin code is C++11.
- **Generated files:** never hand-edit `res/`, `res-src/` or `src/fog/FogLayout.hpp`; run `make panels`.
- **Ids:** slug `Fog`, folder `fog`. Ids are pinned from the first build (Task 6 lists them).
- **Before a task is done:** `make test`. From Task 6 on, also `make` and `make smoke`, and look at `build/screenshots/Fog.png`.

## Review Focus

1. **Fast CV on Size** (a 10 Hz full-range sweep for 10 s) must stay finite and bounded, never read outside a buffer, and never click. The test is `size_cv_sweep_is_safe` (Task 5).
2. **Sample-rate changes during a ringing tail** (44.1 kHz, then 96 kHz, then 48 kHz) give no NaN, and the decay is still correct at the final rate. The test is `rate_change_mid_tail` (Task 5).
3. **A 16-voice ±5 V polyphonic input** stays bounded by the limiter (within ±10.5 V). The test is `poly_input_bounded` (Task 4).
4. **Unplugging IN L while the tail rings** leaves the tail decaying naturally, with no reset and no click. The test is `unplug_keeps_tail` (Task 4).
5. **Dry kill overrides Mix CV:** with dry kill on and Mix CV at −5 V, the output still contains no dry. The test is `dry_kill_overrides_cv` (Task 4).

### Task 2: Building blocks: delay line, diffuser, rate divider

**Files:**
- Create: `src/DelayLine.hpp`, shared by future modules.
- Create: `src/fog/Diffuser.hpp` and `src/fog/RateDivider.hpp`.
- Create: `src/fog/test_blocks.cpp`.
- Modify: `Makefile` (`MODULES += fog`) and `tests.mk` (add `build/test/fog/test_blocks` to `PLUGIN_MATH_TESTS`).

**Interfaces (namespace `rka`):**
- `struct DelayLine`:
  - `void allocate(int maxDelaySamples)` rounds the buffer up to a power of two and zeroes it. It may allocate, and must never be called from `process()`.
  - `void clear()` zeroes the buffer without allocating.
  - `void write(float x)`.
  - `float read(float delaySamples) const` reads with cubic (Hermite) interpolation. The delay is clamped to [1, maxDelay − 2].
  - `float readInt(int delaySamples) const`.
  - `int maxDelay() const`.
- `template <int N> struct Diffuser`:
  - `void allocate(float sampleRate, float maxScale)`;
  - `void configure(float sampleRate, const float* stageMs, int stages, float scale, uint32_t seed)` sets up `stages` stages, each with N delays drawn by seed within that stage's span, a channel shuffle with polarity flips, and a Hadamard mix (N a power of two, up to 4 stages);
  - `void process(float* x)` works on N channels in place;
  - `void clear()`.
- `struct RateDivider`:
  - `void setHostRate(float hz)` picks a factor of 1, 2, 4 or 8 so that hostRate / factor ≤ 96000;
  - `int factor() const` and `float innerRate() const`;
  - `template <class F> void process(const float inL, const float inR, float& outL, float& outR, F inner)` runs `inner(l, r, outL, outR)` at the inner rate, through `src/fathom/Halfband.hpp`'s 2× stages, chained for factor > 2;
  - `void reset()`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `test_blocks.cpp`:
  - `delay_integer_exact`: an impulse comes back exactly at `readInt(d)`, for several d.
  - `delay_fractional_sine`: a 1 kHz sine read at d + 0.37 matches the analytic delayed sine within 1e-3 (48 kHz).
  - `delay_clamps_out_of_range`: `read(-5)` and `read(1e9)` are finite and do not touch memory outside the buffer (run under the sanitizer build too; see Task 5).
  - `delay_power_of_two`: `allocate(1000)` leaves `maxDelay() >= 1000`.
  - `diffuser_preserves_energy`: white noise of RMS 1 on 8 channels through 4 stages keeps the total RMS within 1 % after the delays fill.
  - `diffuser_decorrelates`: an impulse on channel 0 comes out as energy spread over all 8 channels, each holding between 5 % and 25 % of it.
  - `rate_divider_factor`: 44.1k and 96k give 1; 192k gives 2; 384k gives 4; 768k gives 8.
  - `rate_divider_passband`: at 192 kHz, a 5 kHz sine through an identity `inner` comes out within 0.1 dB.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them and see them fail.** Run `make test`. Expected: compile failure, because the headers are missing.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement the three headers.** Use no libm in `process`. Diffuser delays are drawn from a small LCG seeded per stage.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run them and see them pass.** Run `make test`. Expected: `fog test_blocks: all checks passed`. `make test` will also hit the bench guard, because there is no `bench.inc` yet; that is accepted until Task 5. Record the result.

### Task 3: The network (HAAR voicing)

**Files:**
- Create: `src/fog/Voicing.hpp`, `src/fog/Network.hpp` and `src/fog/test_network.cpp`.
- Create: `src/fog/test_measure.hpp`, the test-only measurement helpers.
- Modify: `tests.mk` (add `test_network` to `PLUGIN_MATH_TESTS`).

**Interfaces (namespace `rka::fog`):**
- `enum Mode { HAAR, FRET, SMOKE, ICE, MODES_LEN };`
- `struct Voicing` holds:
  - `int lines` (8 or 16);
  - `float baseMs[16]`;
  - `int diffuseStages` and `float diffuseMs[4]`;
  - `float dampBias` (0 = HAAR);
  - `float bloomBias` (0 = HAAR);
  - `float loopDiffuse` (0 = none; SMOKE);
  - `float shimmer` (0 = none; ICE);
  - `float brightness` (0 = HAAR).
- `const Voicing& voicing(Mode m)`. Only HAAR needs to be tuned in this task; the others may copy HAAR until Task 7.
- `inline float fogT60(float v)` returns `0.4f * 200^v`, via `fastExp2`.
- `struct Network`:
  - `void allocate(float innerRate)`, sized for 3× the longest base length plus the modulation headroom;
  - `void configure(const Voicing&, float innerRate)`;
  - `void clear()`;
  - `void setControls(float visibility, float size, float damp, float drift, int driftRate)`, all in [0, 1], and `driftRate` 0 for Slow or 1 for Very slow;
  - `void process(float inL, float inR, float& wetL, float& wetR)`.
- In `test_measure.hpp`:
  - `double measureT60(const std::vector<float>& ir, float rate, float bandHz)` uses Schroeder backward integration on the IR after a 2nd-order band-pass at `bandHz`, and returns twice the −5 to −35 dB fit (T30 × 2);
  - `double rms(...)` and `double correlation(...)`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `test_network.cpp`. Every expected value comes from the spec or `test_measure.hpp`, never from the engine.
  - `t60_matches_visibility`: HAAR, Size 0.5, Damp 0, Drift 0, at 48 kHz. For each word's v, the T60 at 250 Hz is within ±15 % of `0.4·200^v`. For DENSE, render at least 40 s and use the T30 fit.
  - `t60_holds_across_size`: the T60 at FOG is within ±15 % at Size 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75.
  - `damp_ratio`: T60 at 4 kHz divided by T60 at 250 Hz, with Visibility at FOG, is ≥ 0.8 at Damp 0, between 0.35 and 0.65 at Damp 0.5, and ≤ 0.18 at Damp 1.
  - `even_wet_level`: a held pink-noise input at 2 V RMS. Across the six words, the wet RMS (after 3 × T60, capped at 20 s) stays within ±3 dB of the RMS at v = 0.5, and within ±3 dB of the input RMS.
  - `stereo_decorrelated`: an impulse into L only gives energy on both outputs, with correlation(wetL, wetR) < 0.5.
  - `always_decays`: v = 1, Damp 0, Drift 1. After white noise for 10 s and then silence, the energy over each 5 s window falls monotonically, and is at least 20 dB down after 40 s.
  - `drift_zero_is_static` and `drift_moves`: with Drift 0, two renders of the same input are bit-identical. With Drift 1, a 1 kHz tone in the tail shows a spectral spread wider than with Drift 0.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run them and see them fail.** Run `make test`. Expected: compile failure.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement the network.**
  - The input runs through `Diffuser<8>`, then into the line set. It goes through a Householder matrix for 8 or 16 lines.
  - Each line has a gain `g_i = 10^(-3·len_i / (T60·rate))`, a damping one-pole set from Damp (HF T60 = T60 × the ratio above), a read modulated by Drift (decorrelated slow sines or smoothed noise), and Size slewing.
  - Wet normalisation is a gain from T60, calibrated so `even_wet_level` passes.
  - Use `fastExp2` for all the gain maths, recomputed only when a control changes.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run them and see them pass.** Run `make test`, all Fog tests.
- [ ] **Step 5: Show each test can fail.** Break the per-line T60 gain, so it ignores length, which `t60_holds_across_size` must catch. Remove the damping, which `damp_ratio` must catch. Remove the normalisation, which `even_wet_level` must catch. Restore each.
- [ ] **Step 6: Commit.** `git commit -m "Fog: the network, with time-based decay, damping, drift and an even wet level"`.

### Task 4: The engine

**Files:**
- Create: `src/fog/Bloom.hpp`, `src/fog/FogEngine.hpp` and `src/fog/test_engine.cpp`.
- Modify: `tests.mk` (add `test_engine` to `PLUGIN_MATH_TESTS`).

**Interfaces (namespace `rka`):**
- `struct fog::Bloom` has `void setup(float rate)`, `void set(float bloom, float bias)`, `float process(float x)`, which acts on the signal fed into the network, and `void clear()`.
- `struct FogPort { const float* v = nullptr; int channels = 0; float sum() const; float first() const; }`. `sum()` adds every channel; `first()` is channel 0, or 0 when there is none.
- `struct FogFrame` holds:
  - `enum Param { VISIBILITY, SIZE, DAMP, BLOOM, DRIFT, MIX, SIZE_ATT, DAMP_ATT, BLOOM_ATT, DRIFT_ATT, MIX_ATT, PARAMS_LEN };`
  - `float params[PARAMS_LEN] = {};`
  - `FogPort inL, inR;`
  - `FogPort cv[5];`, in the order Size, Damp, Bloom, Drift, Mix, read with `first()`;
  - `int mode = 0;`, `int driftRate = 0;`, `int lowCut = 1;` (0 Off, 1 50 Hz, 2 100 Hz) and `bool dryKill = false;`.
- `struct FogResult { float outL, outR; };`
- `struct FogEngine`:
  - `void setSampleRate(float hz)` may allocate. It ignores a rate that is not finite or is ≤ 0, keeping the last good one; before any good rate, it uses 48000.
  - `void reset()`.
  - `void process(const FogFrame&, FogResult&)` never allocates.
  - `static float knob(const FogFrame&, int param, int cvIndex, int att)` returns `clamp(param + cv / 10 · att, 0, 1)`. This is Fathom's law: ±5 V at full attenuverter moves the knob by ±0.5, so from noon it sweeps the whole range.

  The engine sanitises every value, applies the low cut and Bloom on the feed, runs the network through `RateDivider`, the limiter and an equal-power Mix, and does the mode-switch fade (the mode-switch tests are Task 7's).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `test_engine.cpp`:
  - `bloom_rise_times`: on `fog::Bloom` with bias 0, a held step reaches within 3 dB of steady in under 50 ms at 0, 1 s ± 30 % at 0.5, and 4 s ± 30 % at 1.
  - `dry_path_zero_delay`: Mix 0.5. A step on the input shows its dry share, `cos(π/4)`, on the very same sample.
  - `mix_law`:
    - Mix 0 is bit-exact dry.
    - Mix 1 has no dry: an impulse's output at sample 0 is 0.
    - Mix 0.5 gives dry and wet gains of `cos(π/4)` and `sin(π/4)` within 1e-6.
  - `dry_kill_overrides_cv`: dry kill on, Mix 0, Mix CV −5 V and att 1. The output at sample 0 for an impulse is 0.
  - `cv_law`: Size 0.5, att 1, CV +5 V gives an effective 1. Att −1 gives 0, and CV 0 gives 0.5. Check through `FogEngine::knob`.
  - `mono_normals`: only IN L connected gives non-zero output on both sides.
  - `poly_input_bounded`: 16-channel ±5 V square waves on IN L and IN R, at v = 1, for 20 s. Every output stays within ±10.5 V.
  - `unplug_keeps_tail`: noise for 2 s, then IN L disconnected (channels 0). The tail keeps decaying with no sample-to-sample step above 0.5 V, and is not zeroed.
  - `low_cut`: a 30 Hz sine's wet RMS is at least 6 dB lower with a 50 Hz low cut than with it Off, and the dry signal is unchanged.
  - `limiter_idle_at_5v`: a ±5 V drone at noon settings gives wet samples identical to running the engine with the limiter disabled (a test-only flag `FogEngine::limiterEnabled`).

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement `Bloom.hpp` and `FogEngine.hpp`.**
- [ ] **Step 4: Run them and see them pass.** Run `make test`.

### Task 7: The other modes, mode switching, then the second listening checkpoint

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/fog/Voicing.hpp`, `src/fog/Network.hpp` and `src/fog/FogEngine.hpp`.
- Create: `src/fog/test_modes.cpp`, and add it to `PLUGIN_MATH_TESTS`.

**Interfaces:** These consume the `Voicing` fields from Task 3:
- **FRET:** `dampBias > 0`, `bloomBias > 0`, and a low-mid emphasis.
- **SMOKE:** fewer, longer lines (8), with `loopDiffuse > 0` (an allpass diffusion stage inside each line's feedback, after the read, as Greyhole puts it).
- **ICE:** `shimmer > 0` (a +12-semitone granular pitch shifter in the feedback, at a fixed low amount; no libm), and `brightness > 0`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - `modes_t60`: in every mode, the T60 at 250 Hz at FOG is within ±20 %.
  - `modes_knob_directions`: in every mode, raising Damp shortens the 4 kHz T60 by at least 30 % from 0 to 1. Raising Bloom lengthens the feed rise time at least 3×.
  - `fret_darker_than_haar`: at the same settings, FRET's 4 kHz/250 Hz T60 ratio is below HAAR's.
  - `smoke_has_echoes`: SMOKE at Size 1, impulse in. The envelope of the wet signal over the first 2 s shows at least 3 distinct peaks at least 6 dB above their neighbouring troughs; HAAR shows fewer.
  - `ice_shimmer`: ICE with a 220 Hz sine in. The tail holds energy near 440 Hz at least 20 dB above HAAR's under the same input.
  - `mode_switch_click_free`: a held drone, switching mode every 1 s through all four modes. No sample-to-sample step exceeds twice the largest step in the preceding 100 ms plus 0.05 V. With silence before the switch, the old tail is below −60 dB within 100 ms.
  - `all_modes_safe`: rerun `always_decays` and `poly_input_bounded` in every mode.
