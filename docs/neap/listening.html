NEAP

Listening list · test build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24 · with Spring

Neap's first test builds: eight slow cycles after the Instruō øchd, each roughly an octave below the one above but on uneven steps so none ever lock, plus TIDE (a weighted sum of all eight, tilted by WEIGHT) and HIGH (a gate while TIDE is above MARK). Spring is its 6 HP expander after øchd's ø4^2: folds, min/max pairs, chained triggers and four 4-bit step codes, all from Neap's cycles. 2.0.39 adds Add Spring expander to Neap's right-click menu. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: the lights, tooltips, the menu item and LINK. RATE's range, the step table, TIDE's gain and the defaults are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

Patches

1. Eight on a scope

Neap ALL → a poly scope (or C1–C4 and C5–C8 on two scopes)

Watch for a few minutes at noon, then at RATE three quarters right, where all eight move visibly. No pair should ever settle into a fixed relationship.

2. A slow drone that breathes

Fathom → Narrows → Sluice → Fog · Neap C3 → Narrows cutoff · C5 → Fathom wavefold or timbre · TIDE → Sluice level CV

The core use: three unrelated slow motions over one sound. Try WEIGHT left and right and listen to how restless TIDE makes the level.

3. Events from HIGH

Neap HIGH → Beacon GATE · Beacon OUT → Sluice level CV on a second layer

A second layer swells in whenever the tide is high. Move MARK to set how often that happens.

4. Spring as a melody

Right-click Neap → Add Spring expander · Spring S1–4 → a quantiser → Fathom V/OCT · Spring T2 → Beacon GATE

A wandering 16-step line, struck by cycle 2's triggers. Then try S odd or S even, and RATE faster.

5. Triggers that fall back

Spring T2 → Rigging STRUM · T8 → Narrows PING (T4 and T6 left empty)

With T4 and T6 unpatched their triggers fall through to T8, so Narrows rings on cycles 4, 6 and 8 together: busier than T8 alone.

6. Folds and pairs into a filter

Spring F1 → Narrows cutoff · MAX 6·7 → Fog size or mix

F1 gives a double-speed, always-positive sweep with a sharp dip; MAX 6·7 a slow, lopsided swell that never falls below the higher of two cycles.
