DOLDRUMS

Listening list · test build 2.0.46 · 2026-11-26 · first build

Doldrums is new: a 30 s tape of the input, held or crept through with PaulStretch's random-phase method, with a tuned comb. Every default, range and rule below is a first guess built from the design, and several rules were decided by the builder while building; this list is where you rule on them. Each check says what to patch, what to do, what you should hear or see if it works as designed, and what your answer decides. The manual (docs/doldrums/README.md) says what everything does; its section "What to expect" lists the limits found while building. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested could not be tried without a live Rack: the drag and its undo, the menu, dark panels, a rate change while running, duplicating a module, the tooltips.

Patches

A. Hold a chord

Rigging (or Fathom) L/R → Doldrums IN L/R · Doldrums L/R → mixer

All knobs as they start. While the chord sounds, press FREEZE: the corner says FROZEN HELD and the chord stays, shimmering, never looping. Press FREEZE again: it moves on from the frozen moment.

B. A tuned drone from noise

Hadal L/R → IN L/R · Shoal (Harrington 1200) B, C, D → VCV Merge → V/OCT

HARMONICS 100 %, ROOT C4 (noon), Grain Long. Trigger Harrington's moves slowly: each chord dissolves in over about a grain.

C. A slow scan

Neap cycle 5 → Position CV (Position at noon, attenuverter fully right)

A changing 30 s on the tape; STRETCH at ∞, then FREEZE (the freeze puts the slice on the anchor). Neap scans the anchor back and forth across the whole tape; each grain takes the slice where it is.

D. Eddy → Doldrums → Eddy

Eddy A L/R → IN L/R · Doldrums L/R → Eddy B IN L/R · RUN → Eddy B REC · a trigger → TRIG

Step by step in the manual's "Patches to try". In short: Stop in the menu and wait for STOPPED; Position a little more than the clip's length back; play the clip; Freeze as it ends (FROZEN STOPPED, silent); drag the anchor to the clip's start; patch RUN to Eddy B's REC while it is low; one trigger. Eddy B records exactly one pass, fades included.
