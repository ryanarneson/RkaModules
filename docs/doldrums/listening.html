DOLDRUMS

Listening list · test build 2.0.46 · 2026-11-26 · first build

Doldrums is new: a 30 s tape of the input, held or crept through with PaulStretch's random-phase method, with a tuned comb. Every default, range and rule below is a first guess built from the design, and several rules were decided by the builder while building; this list is where you rule on them. Each check says what to patch, what to do, what you should hear or see if it works as designed, and what your answer decides. The manual (docs/doldrums/README.md) says what everything does; its section "What to expect" lists the limits found while building. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested could not be tried without a live Rack: the press and drag in the window and their undo, a rate change while running, duplicating a module, the tooltips, Initialize and its undo.

Since 2.0.46 (from your listening and the final review): Position starts about 3 s back, so Freeze at the defaults loops the last few seconds and the instant-hold is Position fully right; the corner says FROZEN AT END when Stretch has nothing to act on; the read head is drawn as a gold bar; a thaw goes back to the Position flag at once; Stretch brought back to 1 rejoins the flag; a press in the window plays from there; in Stop, Freeze always waits for TRIG unless it holds; the comb's loudness is measured from the sound; Initialize no longer clicks onto the tape. The window's marks, named as the checks use them: the Position flag (a thin cyan line with a small solid triangle on top: where a pass starts), the gold bar (the read head: where the module has got to), the slice (a short cyan bar along the bottom with a faint band above it: the piece of tape the current grain is made from), the bright and dim peaks (bright: what the pass has still to play) and the word in the top right corner. The manual's "The tape and its window" has them in full.

Patches

A. Hold a chord

Rigging (or Fathom) L/R → Doldrums IN L/R · Doldrums L/R → mixer

At the defaults, press FREEZE while the chords play: FROZEN, the last 3 s drawn out and looped. Then turn POSITION fully right and press FREEZE twice (off, then on while a chord sounds): the corner says FROZEN AT END and the chord stays, shimmering, never looping; the flag and the gold bar stand together at the right edge. Press FREEZE again: the gold bar goes back to the flag (now) at once; at x8 you then hear the input from that moment on, slowed, falling behind (at x1 it would be the live input).

B. A tuned drone from noise

Hadal L/R → IN L/R · Shoal (Harrington 1200) B, C, D → VCV Merge → V/OCT

HARMONICS 100 %, ROOT C4 (noon), Grain Long. Trigger Harrington's moves slowly: each chord dissolves in over about a grain.

C. A slow scan

Neap cycle 5 → Position CV (Position at noon, attenuverter fully right)

A changing 30 s on the tape; STRETCH at ∞, then FREEZE (the freeze puts the slice on the Position flag: FROZEN HELD). Neap scans the flag back and forth across the whole tape, the gold bar and the slice with it; each grain takes the slice where it is.

D. Eddy → Doldrums → Eddy

Eddy A L/R → IN L/R · Doldrums L/R → Eddy B IN L/R · RUN → Eddy B REC · a trigger → TRIG

Step by step in the manual's "Patches to try". In short: choose Stop in the menu (no need to wait); type Position a little more than the clip's length back; play the clip; Freeze as it ends (FROZEN STOPPED, silent, RUN low, at once); put the Position flag on the clip's start with the Position knob (a press in the window would play from there); patch RUN to Eddy B's REC while it is low; one trigger. Eddy B records exactly one pass, fades included.
