FATHOM

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Fathom is a bass drone voice, 10 HP: a sine sub under a seven-saw body that drifts slowly, through a Moog-style ladder filter with resonance and drive. One V/OCT input, stereo L and R outputs and a separate SUB output. It is mono: one voice. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: the tooltips, the right-click menu and how the panel looks. The defaults, the drift amount, the drive makeup and the self-oscillation level are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

THE DEFAULTSC2 (65.4 Hz), SUB 50 %, DETUNE 25 %, SPREAD 60 %, DRIFT 25 %, CUTOFF 1.2 kHz, RES 20 %, DRIVE 25 %. At these the output peaks near 4 V with mild saturation. Is that the right place to start for dark ambient, or too bright, too wide or too loud?
DRIFTAt 100 % each saw wanders up to ±6 cents and 3 dB, with a new target about twice a second slewed over a second. Held for minutes, is 25 % the right amount, or is the wander too quick for a drone?
SUBA sine one octave below PITCH by default (two in the menu), joining after the filter so CUTOFF never thins it. Is that the right default? Would you rather it went through the filter?
SELF-OSCILLATIONAt the top of RES the ladder sings a quiet sine, about 1 V at the jack, 10 to 12 dB under the drone, tracking CUTOFF at 1 V/oct. Quiet enough, or should it be louder? The sine fades as DRIVE rises.
DRIVE AND LEVELDRIVE runs from half to eight times gain (unity at 25 %), with makeup so the level stays within about 2 dB. Does the whole sweep sound like tone change rather than level change?
PITCH RANGEPITCH is ±2 octaves, snapped to semitones by default, with 0 at C2. V/OCT adds on top; the result is held between 8 Hz and 4 kHz. Is the snap right by default, and is ±2 octaves enough?
MONO (issue 64)Only channel 1 of V/OCT and the CVs is read, so a chord cable plays one note. Open issue 64 asks for a voice per channel; the filter is 2x oversampled, so CPU grows with voices. Wanted?
CUTOFF RANGE20 Hz to about 20 kHz (the knob spans 10 octaves from 20 Hz, so the top is 20.48 kHz). Is the knob easy to set in the low, dark part?

Patches

1. Plain drone

Fathom L → Fog Left · Fathom R → Fog Right · Fog out to the speakers

Nothing else is patched. Listen for several minutes at the defaults: it should keep slowly breathing. Then DRIFT to 0 and compare.

2. Sub on its own bus

Fathom SUB → Sluice A · Fathom L → Sluice B · Fathom R → Sluice C

SUB OUT is the raw sine before the SUB knob, the filter and the clipper, so row A stays a clean sub whatever the voice does. Turn SUB down to 0 and the L / R sub goes while SUB OUT stays.

3. Slow filter wander

Sway RAND (Range Slow, SMOOTH fully left) → Fathom CUTOFF CV · Fathom L → Fog Left

Set the CUTOFF attenuverter to about +40 %. The cutoff glides between random values over a cycle. 1 V per octave at full attenuverter.

4. Swell on a gate

Squall GATE → Beacon GATE · Beacon OUT → Sluice A level CV · Fathom L → Sluice A

Each gate swells the drone in and lets it fade, as in the Beacon list. SHAPE fully left on Beacon for a slow start.

5. Growl

Sway SIN (Slow range) → Fathom CUTOFF CV · Fathom L, R → Narrows Left, Right

Raise RES to about 60 % and DRIVE to about 70 % with Filter type Low-pass 24 dB. Sweep CUTOFF slowly: the peak sits a little below CUTOFF as the stages saturate.

6. Stepped pitch

Fundamental SEQ3 CV → Fathom V/OCT · Fathom L → Fog Left

V/OCT steps are smoothed over 5 ms, so no click. Add Free pitch in the menu to tune between semitones.
