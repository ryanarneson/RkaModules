WAKE

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Wake is a stereo echo, 14 HP, for sounds that have just been struck: a delay of 10 ms to 10 s with feedback, a tone filter in the loop, a clock input, freeze and ping pong, in three styles (Tape, Analog, Digital). Diffuse blurs the repeats and Drift makes the echo time waver. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: the lights, the tooltips, how the panel looks and the module browser. The defaults, the three styles and the Diffuse and Drift amounts are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

DEFAULTSTime 300 ms, Feedback 40 %, Tone flat, Ratio 1:1, Mix 35 %, Diffuse 0, Drift 15 %, Tape. Keep, or start slower and darker (longer Time, Tone a little left, some Diffuse) for ambient and dark work?
TIME RANGE10 ms to 10 s on a log scale, opening at 300 ms. Is 10 ms short enough and 10 s long enough, and is the middle (100 ms to 3 s) where you want the room?
FEEDBACK TOPThe knob goes to 110 %, with a soft ceiling so it holds a saturated wash. Is 110 % the right top, and is the 100 mark by the knob easy to find?
TONE RANGEDarkest about 500 Hz and thinnest about 1 kHz per pass. Too little or too much at either end?
THE THREE STYLESTape: 6 kHz top, bend time 0.15 s. Analog: 5 kHz falling to 1.5 kHz at long times, harder drive, bend time 0.08 s. Digital: clean, crossfade 40 ms. Are the three far enough apart, and is the pitch bend's speed right?
DIFFUSE AND DRIFTDiffuse smears each pass; Drift is up to 15 cents a side (30 apart), 15 % by default. Is Diffuse enough at full to become a wash, and is Drift too little, too much, or better off by default?
RATIO AND CLOCK STEPSSeven ratio steps (1/2, 2/3, 3/4 of Time on either side) and ten clock multiples (1/4 to x4). Is any step missing or never used?
FREEZE HOLDS TIMEWhile frozen, Time, Ratio, the clock and Ping Pong are held until release. Fine, or should Time still move the held loop?

Patches

1. Depth behind a strum

Squall GATE → Rigging STRUM · Rigging L, R → Wake IN L, IN R · Wake L, R → Ballast

Tape, TIME about 400 ms, FEEDBACK 40 %, RATIO one step right, MIX 30 %. Each strike trails off to one side.

2. Echoes on the clock

Squall GATE → Rigging STRUM and → Wake CLOCK

The same clock drives both. Turn TIME to x1.5 or 3/4 and the echoes land between the hits. Watch the tooltip read the step.

3. A bounce

Rigging L → Wake IN L · Wake L, R → Ballast

PING PONG on, RATIO two steps left, FEEDBACK 60 %, Digital. One hit becomes a long-short pattern crossing the field.

4. A dark wash

Hadal L, R → Wake IN L, IN R · Wake L, R → Fog Left, Right

Analog, TIME 2 s, FEEDBACK 85 %, DIFFUSE 70 %, TONE a little left, MIX about 50 %. Hadal dissolves into a dark cloud; Fog makes it bigger.

5. Hold a moment

Squall GATE → Wake FREEZE jack · Rigging L → Wake IN L

A slow gate freezes what was passing and holds it until the gate ends. Try DRIFT at 0 and at 15 % while it is held.

6. Tape slur

Sway Sine → Wake TIME CV · Fathom Left → Wake IN L

Tape, FEEDBACK 70 %, the TIME CV attenuverter barely open. The repeats bend around the note.

7. Self-sustaining drone

Rigging L → Wake IN L · Wake L → Narrows IN L

FEEDBACK above 100 % with a short TIME and TONE left: one hit becomes a held tone at the echo's pitch. Narrows on the way out (Ladder, LP) darkens it. Bring Feedback down to let it go.
