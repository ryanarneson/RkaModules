BEACON

Listening list · test build 2.0.34 · 2026-11-23

Beacon's first test build: a polyphonic envelope after the Instruō cèis, with Gate / Trig / Hold on a switch, a gate for every stage, a stage trigger, OUT and INV, Shape from swell through linear to classic, and a window that draws the envelope. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: everything you see in the window, the lights and the button. The defaults and Shape's ends are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

Verdicts (first guesses; say what to change)

WhatThe question
SHAPEFully right is classic (a fast, rounded attack; decays with a long tail); fully left the swell (a slow-starting attack; decays that hold, then fall). Default is three quarters right (+50 %). Are the ends too strong or too weak, and is the default right?
DEFAULT TIMESAttack 10 ms, Decay 300 ms, Sustain 5 V, Release 1 s. Keep, or start slower for ambient work?
SHORT TIMESThe knobs run 1 ms to 60 s, each decade an equal share of the turn; noon is about 245 ms. Is the bottom of the knob (1 to 50 ms) easy enough to set?
THE WINDOWStage widths grow with the log of their time, not to scale. Does it read at a glance? Is the dot easy to follow? In Hold it shows a scrolling line of the output instead: useful, or would two marks (input and output) be better?
WINDOW GLOWThe window's lines, dim ones included, are drawn as light, as Eddy's are, so they still show with Rack's room lights turned down. Keep, or dim the inactive stages more in the dark?
SHAPE TOOLTIPIt reads "Linear" only at exactly noon; a drag that stops just beside noon reads a small percentage (e.g. 0.4 %). Fine, or snap a small zone round noon?

Patches

1. Plain voice

MIDI to CV → Fathom V/OCT · GATE → Beacon GATE · Beacon OUT → Sluice level CV and Narrows cutoff

The first test: play notes. Switch on GATE. Turn A through its range, then D, S and R. Then SHAPE fully left, noon and fully right on the same settings and listen to how the same times feel.

2. Legato with Retrig

MIDI to CV GATE → GATE · RETRIG → RETRIG

Play overlapping notes. Without RETRIG patched, only the first note gets an Attack. With it, every new note climbs from where the envelope is back to the top, then decays to Sustain, and held notes stay connected.

3. Slow swells for a drone

Squall GATE → Beacon GATE (Trig) · OUT → Sluice level CV · Fathom → Sluice → Fog

Switch on TRIG. A at about 8 s, R at 20 s, SHAPE fully left. Each Squall gate swells the drone in and lets it fade. Then try Shape fully right on the same times.

4. Events from the stage gates

Beacon A gate → Rigging STRUM · Beacon TRIG → Narrows PING

One envelope drives events: Rigging is struck when Attack begins (the A gate rising), and Narrows pings at every stage start. A long gate gives five pings: at the start of Attack, Decay, Sustain and Release, and at the return to rest.

5. Ducking with INV

Beacon INV → Sluice level CV (a drone already open)

INV runs 0 to −10 V, so it pulls the drone down while the envelope rises and lets it back as it falls. Triggered by Squall or a clock, the drone breathes around the hits.

6. Hold as glide

Sequencer V/OCT → Beacon SUSTAIN CV · Beacon OUT → Fathom V/OCT

Switch on HOLD and turn S fully left (no offset). A sets the glide up, D the glide down. Watch the A, D and S gates: A while it glides up, D down, S when it has arrived.

7. Polyphonic chord

MIDI to CV (4 voices) GATE → Beacon GATE · OUT → Sluice (poly) · V/OCT → a poly oscillator

Each voice gets its own envelope; staggered notes each start their own Attack. The window and the stage lights follow voice 1.

8. Retrig pulses on a held sound

Push button held (or a long gate) · Squall GATE or a clock → RETRIG

Hold the gate high and send pulses into RETRIG: the envelope re-swells from wherever it is. D and S set how far each pulse falls back before the next.
