BEACON

Listening list · test build 2.0.45 · 2026-11-25 · a full retest

Beacon back for a full retest, on one rule: it should always do what you expect, and the window should always show what it is doing. What changed (2.0.44 and 2.0.45): the switch reads ADSR / AD / HOLD; AD is a one-shot, as a trigger into Maths (Attack to the top, then down over the Decay time, whatever the gate does); an Attack that starts part-way up picks up its curve and takes only the time left; the window draws what the mode will do, each stage in its own zone. The manual's new table, "Gates, pulses and triggers", says what each gate length gives in each mode. The retest report (beacon-2.0.45-retest.html) shows 22 of these patches as scope traces beside the window. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested need your eyes: the window's geometry is now tested against the output every sample, but not how it looks in Rack, nor the switch, the lights and the button.

Patches

1. Gate length in ADSR

Sway square (PW knob) → Beacon GATE · Beacon OUT → a scope and Sluice level CV

ADSR, A 50 ms, D 200 ms, S 5 V. Sway at 1 Hz, then sweep its PW from 1 % (10 ms) up to 50 %: a blip, then up and part-way down Decay, then the full ADSR holding at Sustain. The gate's length is shaping the envelope, as on any ADSR.

2. The same pulses in AD

the same patch · switch to AD

Now every pulse, from 1 % to 99 %, gives the same envelope: up to 10 V in 50 ms, then down over the 200 ms Decay. Change Sway's rate and PW: nothing about the shape changes. The window shows only A and D. (Or use Sway's EOC, a true 1 ms trigger.)

3. Retriggers

MIDI to CV GATE → GATE · its RETRIG → RETRIG

ADSR, A 1 s, S 6 V. Hold a note into Sustain, then play overlapping notes: each climbs from 6 V and reaches the top in less than a full second. Watch the dot join the drawn Attack curve at the level and ride it up. Try Shape fully left, noon and fully right.

4. Fast triggers into a long Attack

a clock at 8 to 16 Hz → GATE · AD

A 500 ms, D 300 ms. The envelope climbs steadily to the top in half a second, then falls a little between triggers and catches the top again. Before 2.0.44 it crept and never arrived.

5. Slow swells for a drone

Squall GATE → Beacon GATE (AD) · OUT → Sluice level CV · Fathom → Sluice → Fog

A about 8 s, D 20 s, SHAPE fully left. Each Squall gate swells the drone in and lets it fade, however long Squall holds its gate. A new gate mid-fade swells back up from where it is.

6. Events from the stage gates

Beacon A gate → Rigging STRUM · Beacon TRIG → Narrows PING

ADSR with a long gate: five pings (Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release, rest). AD: three (Attack, Decay, rest).

7. Hold as glide

Sequencer V/OCT → Beacon SUSTAIN CV · Beacon OUT → Fathom V/OCT

Unchanged in this build. HOLD, S fully left. A sets the glide up, D the glide down.
