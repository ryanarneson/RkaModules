Verdicts (kept as built at 2.0.29; say if any is wrong)

WhatThe question
THE WINDOWThe recording is fixed on the glass and the record head sweeps across it (as Eddy's window). The alternative is a scrolling view, newest at the right. Which reads better?
DEFAULTSA new Spindrift starts at Density 0.65 (random side) and Dry/Wet at half, so it makes a cloud at once. Beads' own panel has no defaults. Keep these, or start dry?
QUALITYThe setting is spelt out in the window, not shown by a coloured light as on Beads. Enough, or do you want lights on the panel (about 2 HP more)?
LEVELSBeads' full scale is ±10 V, so ±5 V audio sits at half scale and fully dry comes out at 0.75 of the input, as the hardware does, in Digital, Clean Tape and Vintage Digital (Vintage 0.747 to 0.750; Clean Tape drops from about 6.8 V peak). In Dirty Tape it is lower and depends on level: 0.74 at 0.5 V peak, 0.69 at 5 V, 0.57 at 10 V. Does it sit right in your patches next to the other RKA modules?

Patches

1. Frozen cloud bed

Fathom → Spindrift → Fog · Sway → TIME CV

A slow Fathom drone in. Digital, DENSITY at 2 o'clock, SIZE at 1 o'clock, SHAPE at noon, Time's attenuverter a little clockwise for scatter. After ten seconds press FREEZE; now a very slow Sway into TIME CV walks the cloud through the frozen buffer. Fog after it, mostly wet. Leave it for ten minutes.

2. Dirty-tape choir

Squall → Rigging → Spindrift → Fog

Squall strikes Rigging's strings sparsely. Spindrift on DIRTY TAPE, PITCH at +12 with its attenuverter a touch clockwise (each grain a little detuned), FEEDBACK at 10 o'clock, REVERB at noon, DRY/WET nearly full. The struck notes smear into a wobbly, hissing chorus.

3. Reverse shimmer

Any pad → Spindrift → Narrows (low-pass)

SIZE below 9 o'clock so grains play backwards, PITCH at +12 or +7, FEEDBACK at 11 o'clock so each pass climbs again, SHAPE towards reverse for swelling grains. Narrows after it tames the top. The classic shimmer, but made of reversed grains.

4. Clocked grains

Squall gates (or Shoal clock) → SEED · Hadal → IN L

Seed latched (its light breathing) with a clock in SEED: Density now divides the clock (left of noon, /1 to /16) or thins it by probability (right). Hadal's rumble as the source gives rhythmic bursts of texture. Turn Density through the dead zone and listen for the change of behaviour.

5. Delay as a rhythm machine

Shoal clock → SEED · Rigging → Spindrift

SIZE fully clockwise for delay mode (it crosses over in about 1.5 s). With a clock in SEED, TIME picks a ratio of the clock and DENSITY divides the clock (left of noon, /1 to /16) or thins it by probability (right of noon), which sets which clock steps give a repeat; PITCH changes the tape speed. Feedback up for long tails, VINTAGE for a grittier echo.

6. The hidden synth

Shoal or a sequencer → V/OCT · Sway → TIME CV · nothing in IN L/R

Unplug both inputs and wait ten seconds (or menu → Synth mode → Always). FEEDBACK chooses one of twelve wavetable banks, TIME the wave, DRY/WET blends the steady voice with a cloud of wavetable grains. A slow Sway on TIME sweeps the timbre. Try each QUALITY for 4-bit to 16-bit crunch.

7. Granulated field recording

Eddy (field recording, looped) → Spindrift → Wake → Fog

Eddy plays a few seconds of a field recording on loop. Spindrift on CLEAN TAPE with a long buffer (10.7 s), DENSITY high and random, SIZE long, Time's attenuverter well clockwise: the recording becomes a slowly churning cloud. Wake and Fog after it for distance.

8. CV-driven randomness

Sway → SIZE CV (attenuverter anticlockwise)

With a cable in, an attenuverter turned anticlockwise makes the CV's positive half set how much randomness the grains get. A slow Sway then breathes the cloud between orderly and scattered. Try it on PITCH too, for passages of detuned grains.
