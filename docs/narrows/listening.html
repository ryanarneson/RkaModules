NARROWS

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Narrows is a polyphonic stereo filter, 14 HP, with a Ladder (24 dB) and a State (12 dB) core, a Mode dial from low-pass through band-pass to high-pass, resonance that self-oscillates, a comb, drive and spread. It can filter anything, or be struck with a trigger at PING or played as a sine voice from V/OCT. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: the tooltips, how the panel looks and the module browser. The defaults, Drive's range, the comb's level and the Mode in-betweens are first guesses, to be set by ear here.

Verdicts (first guesses; say what to change)

WhatThe question
CUTOFF16 Hz to 16.4 kHz, an octave per tenth of the turn, opening at 1 kHz. Is 1 kHz the right place to start, or should it open lower for dark work?
DEFAULT COREThe switch opens up on Ladder (24 dB). Keep, or should State (12 dB, more open) be the default?
SELF-OSCILLATIONRes sings from 95 % and reaches about 4 to 5 V at 100 %, within 7 cents of pitch up to about 3.5 kHz (above 4 kHz it drifts, sharp in the state core and flat in the ladder). Is the last 5 % of the knob enough to set the level, and is the sine too loud or too quiet?
COMBPeaks reach about 16 dB over dry at full, and Left puts them halfway between, an octave lower. Right level? Does the left half sound as useful as the right?
DRIVEUp to 24 dB of gain, with 62 % of that (in octaves) taken back at the output. Is the range right, and does the level stay even as you turn it?
MODEA half-turn dial, LP at the left end, blends between neighbours, with its own blend in each core. Does the sweep feel even, and is LP the right default?
SPREADHalf an octave each way at full. Enough for width, or should it go further?
CV SCALESRes and Mode CV take 10 V for the whole travel; Comb CV takes 5 V either way; FM is 1 V per octave at full. Is the mix of scales a problem when patching?

Patches

1. Dark water

Hadal L, R → Narrows IN L, IN R · Sway Sine → Narrows FM · Narrows L, R → Wake IN L, IN R

Ladder, LP, CUTOFF around 300 Hz, RES 40 %. Open FM a little so the slow Sway moves the cutoff.

2. Dark to thin

Sway Triangle → Narrows MODE CV · Hadal Hiss → Narrows IN L

A slow Sway turns the sound from low-pass through band-pass to high-pass and back. Try both cores.

3. A tuned ping

Squall Gate → Narrows PING · Squall Step → Narrows V/OCT · Narrows L, R → Wake IN L, IN R

Nothing in the audio inputs. RES 90 %. Each gate rings the filter close to the pitch of the step, not exactly on it: at RES 90 % the state core rings about 5 cents flat and the ladder about 49 cents flat (nearly a quarter of a semitone). The ring reaches the pitch as RES nears 95 %. Rings above about 1.5 kHz are weak, because the 1 ms ping has little energy there. Wake follows it.

4. A sine voice

Squall Step → Narrows V/OCT · Narrows L → Sluice input · Squall Gate → Sluice level CV

RES 100 %, nothing into the audio inputs. Sluice shapes the loudness.

5. A chord of filters

Hadal L → Narrows IN L · 4-voice poly V/OCT → Narrows V/OCT · Narrows L, R → Fog Left, Right

RES 85 %, band-pass. The noise is tuned to the chord, one filter per voice. Narrows sends four channels on each output; use a mixer that sums them before Fog.

6. Width from mono

Fathom Left → Narrows IN L · Narrows L, R → Ballast channel 1 left, right

SPREAD 30 %, with a little COMB to the right. A mono sound opens across the field.

7. After Rigging

Rigging L, R → Narrows IN L, IN R · Narrows L, R → Fog Left, Right

State core, band-pass, DRIVE 40 %. The strings are narrowed to a hollow band, then given room by Fog.
