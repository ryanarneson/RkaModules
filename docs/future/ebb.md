# Ebb: mutes and slow fades

Status: idea, 2026-11-26. Not designed; no sketch yet. The name is a
working title and has not been checked against the VCV Library. Added
when the user asked what the roadmap was missing. Ballast's manual has
said since it was built that mute, solo and fades "may follow".

**What it is.** A button for each channel that brings it in or takes it
out over a set time, from a click-free instant to minutes. Pressing it
during a fade turns the fade round from where it is. A gate input does
the same as the button, so Watch or Squall can bring a layer in.

**The name.** The ebb: the tide going out. A fade out is an ebb, a fade
in is the flow.

**Why Sluice and Beacon do not cover it.** A Beacon into a Sluice
channel is a fade, at two modules and a cable per channel and with no
button that shows whether the channel is in or out. Bringing layers in
and out by hand over a twenty-minute piece is the commonest gesture in
this music and should be one press.

**Sketched controls.** Not drawn. Two forms are possible:
- an expander for Ballast (as Pan is), 4 to 6 HP: four lit buttons, four
  gate inputs, one Time knob (or Rise and Fall); it acts on Ballast's
  channels, so no audio jacks;
- a standalone module with its own four stereo ins and outs, usable
  without Ballast.

**Open questions.** Expander or standalone (the expander is smaller and
is what the manual promised); one time for all channels or one each;
separate times in and out; the fade's curve (equal loudness per second,
so a two-minute fade does not seem to finish in the first thirty
seconds); whether the gate input is level (high is in) or toggles on
each trigger; solo; whether the button's light shows the fade's
progress (it should: the panel must say what is heard); whether a fade
state is saved with the patch.
