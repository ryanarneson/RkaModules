# Lull: a slew limiter

Status: idea, 2026-11-15. Not designed. One of six 2 HP utilities.

![mockup](lull-mockup.svg)

**What it is.** Smooths a stepped voltage, with separate times for rising and falling.

**The name.** A lull: the wind easing.

**Sketched controls.** Rise and Fall knobs, an input, a light, an output.

**Open questions.** Time ranges; linear or exponential curves; CV over the times, which would not fit at 2 HP; overlap with Squall's Slew.
