FOG

Listening list · build 2.0.39 · 2026-11-24

Fog is a stereo reverb, 10 HP, for large dark spaces: decay is set in seconds on the Visibility knob (0.4 s to 80 s, and it holds at any Size), with Size, Damp, Bloom, Drift and Mix on knobs that each have an attenuverter and CV, and four modes (HAAR, FRET, SMOKE, ICE) in the right-click menu. Its measured comparison against ValhallaRoom LV-426 is a separate testbed, on the share in fog-testbed/; this list is for listening. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested are paths no automated run could reach: the panel and knob faces, the tooltips, the menus and presets, and Rack's CPU meter. The defaults, the four modes and the factory presets are first guesses (issue 23), to be set by ear here.

Verdicts (first guesses; say what to change)

WhatThe question
VISIBILITY RANGET60 = 0.4 s × 200^v: 0.4 s fully left, about 5.7 s at noon, 80 s fully right, with the words CLEAR to DENSE on the face. Are the ends right for dark ambient, and is noon the right default?
DEFAULTSEvery knob opens at noon, every attenuverter at 0, Mix at 50/50, Mode HAAR. For ambient work, would you start with more Mix, a longer Visibility, or more Damp?
WET LEVELThe wet follows the decay time, as in ValhallaRoom: CLEAR is about 11.5 dB quieter than noon and a long tail is louder, with a leveller turning loud drones down. Does the loudness feel right as Visibility moves, or would you rather it stayed flat?
BLOOMFully left is under 50 ms, noon about 1 s, fully right about 4 s on the signal fed in (1.2 to 1.7 s measured at the output at noon). Is noon the right swell, and is 4 s long enough at the top?
THE MODESHAAR dense wash, FRET dark and slow, SMOKE distinct echoes, ICE sparse with an octave up. Each mode's character, equal loudness between them and the factory presets (Becalmed, Nebula, Harbour, Whiteout) have not been tuned by ear (issue 23). What would you change?
EARLY SOUNDFog has no early reflections: after a gate opens its output takes about 0.18 s to reach −20 dB of its level, against 0.02 s for LV-426 (issue 21). Does a pluck or a drum feel late, or is the slow arrival what you want?
BYPASS WITH ONE INPUTBypass wires L to L and R to R, so with only IN L patched OUT R goes silent, although the module normals R to L when active (issue 13). Should bypass normal R to L as well?
MENU DEFAULTSLow cut 50 Hz, Drift rate Slow (about 0.1 to 0.5 Hz), Dry kill off. Is 50 Hz the right cut for sub-bass drones, and is Slow the right drift rate, or would you start with Very slow?

Patches

1. First wash

Fathom Left → Fog IN L · Fog OUT L and OUT R → the speakers

Leave every knob at noon. Hold a note and let go: a HAAR wash, 50/50 with the dry, that outlasts the note by several seconds. IN R is empty, so both sides get the left.

2. Dark held drone

Hadal Left → Fog IN L · Sway Sine → Fog Size CV

Mode FRET, Visibility at THICK, Damp up, Bloom up. Turn the Size attenuverter up a little, with the Sway very slow: the space breathes. Then try a fast Sway on Size and hear it as a pitch effect.

3. Fog on a send

Fathom Left → Ballast Channel 1 left and → Fog IN L · Fog OUT L → Ballast Channel 2 left · Fog OUT R → Ballast Channel 2 right

Turn on Dry kill (wet only) in the menu: Fog is all wet and Ballast sets the balance. Mix stays where it was.

4. Breathing into the tail

Fathom Left → Sluice A · Sway Sine → Sluice A CV · Sluice A → Fog IN L

Sluice A Level at noon. The drone fades in and out; the tail keeps going through the quiet parts. Try Visibility near FOG and near CLEAR.

5. Plucks in a long space

Squall Gate → Rigging STRUM · Rigging Left and Right → Fog IN L and IN R

Mode ICE, Visibility near FOG, Mix at about 30 %. Each strum rings and blurs into an octave-up wash; compare HAAR and SMOKE on the same strums.

6. Slow motion on three knobs

Neap C4 → Fog Damp CV · Neap C6 → Fog Drift CV · Neap C8 → Fog Bloom CV

Turn the three attenuverters up to about 50 %. Three unrelated slow movements over one tail; the knobs at noon give ±5 V the whole range.

7. Echoes into the wash

Fathom Left → Wake Left in · Wake Left out → Fog IN L

Wake's echoes arrive in the reverb, which smears them. Then try SMOKE mode for echoes that stay distinct.
