Listening list for the fixes · build 2.0.51 · 2026-11-27

A short list for what build 2.0.51 changed in Fog, after the code review of 2.0.50 (issue 123, with issues 15 and 21). Each check says what to do, what you should hear now, and what it did before. The whole-module list is fog-2.0.51-listening.html; the detail and the measurements are in Fog-2.0.51-Technical-Reference.md. Ticks and notes are kept in this browser; Copy notes puts them on the clipboard to paste back. Items marked untested could not be reached by any automated run and are being tried here for the first time.

What changed

Long tails keep their top at 44.1 and 48 kHz.The delay lines lost highs on every pass, so a long tail went dark whatever Damp said, and darker at 48 kHz than at 96 kHz. At 48 kHz, Damp fully left, the 12 kHz decay was 3.3 to 4.6 s for 5.66 s, and 10.5 s for 36 s. Now every band is within 3 % of Visibility's time.
HAAR, FRET and ICE have early reflections.The wet starts 2 to 4 ms after the sound and is within 20 dB of its level in about 0.03 s. It took 0.12 s (HAAR), 0.15 s (FRET) and 0.08 s (ICE). SMOKE has none.
Bypass empties the reverb.Releasing bypass starts clean; the old tail does not come back. Bypass with one input still reaches both outputs.
The low cut does not thump when it is switched back on.
The wet level is worked out a new way.The law is the same (level follows decay time). It is met more closely: within 0.5 dB at every Visibility and Size, and the modes within 0.2 dB of each other.
SMOKE's late tail decays at the set rate.At small Size the last of the tail was 10 to 14 % slow.
High sample rates:Bloom opens fully and keeps its times; the leveller releases all the way; the tone stage in FRET and ICE is quiet.

Two patches

F. Bursts and clicks

Hadal Left → Sluice A · Squall Gate → Sluice A CV · Sluice A → Fog IN L · Fog OUT L and OUT R → the speakers

A short, bright sound with silence after it: Sluice opened by a short gate over Hadal's noise. Any hi-hat, rim shot or click will do instead. Keep the bursts short (under a tenth of a second) and well apart, so each tail and each onset is heard alone. Set Fog's Mix fully right and Bloom fully left unless a check says otherwise.

D. Drone and notes

Fathom Left → Fog IN L · Fog OUT L and OUT R → the speakers

Held notes and chords from Fathom, for the checks that need a steady sound or a low one.
